Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use CureAI, we collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, update your profile, or communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, and password.

We also collect the medical symptom descriptions and chat history you provide to the AI assistant. This is necessary to provide the core functionality of our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, to process your requests, and to communicate with you. Your chat history is processed by our AI models to generate medical insights. We do not use your personal health data to train public models.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
